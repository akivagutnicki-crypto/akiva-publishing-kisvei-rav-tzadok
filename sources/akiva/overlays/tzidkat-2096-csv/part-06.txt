Kisvei Rav Tzadok Parallel — Kometz HaMinchah 2:27 (§10): “And the Torah exists from the perspective of Creation; through this there is room for human actions, service, and free will, and this emerges via contraction tzimtzum; and therefore in the entire narrative of Creation, only the Name Elohim is mentioned. And only half the Name indicating ""He was"" and ""He will be"" is read, the letters Yud-Hei, while the second half indicating ""He is"" the present is not read as written, for the divine reality is not openly visible, but rather the opposite—leaving free choice to man.”
