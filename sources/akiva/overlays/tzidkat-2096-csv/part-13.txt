Kisvei Rav Tzadok Parallel — Sichat Malakhei HaSharet 2:20 (§3): “And the Midrash notes that ""the Almighty spread His cloud...""; the Name Shaddai is elucidated elsewhere in our writings as denoting the contraction (tzimtzum) of the manifest Shechinah to mortal capacity, as in Chagigah (12a): ""for I said to the world: Enough (dai) ""; meaning that divine revelation should not expand boundlessly, which would blind and overwhelm finite recipients; this Name hinting at the limiting boundary Hashem set so His light is measured to human capacity, which is the purpose of creation.”
