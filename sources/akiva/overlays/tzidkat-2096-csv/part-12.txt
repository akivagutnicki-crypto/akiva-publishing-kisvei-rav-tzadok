Kisvei Rav Tzadok Parallel — Sefer HaZikhronot 18:2: “And know that the author of Halakhot Gedolot did not count ""Hear, O Israel"" as a distinct commandment of Unity, and the Rashbatz in Zohar HaRakia [12] wrote that Behag views this as an axiomatic belief like ""I am"", and that what is formally counted is the recitation of the Shema; see there; and so it is in Sefer Yere'im which follows Behag [Section 13], enumerating ""Hear, O Israel"" as the command to recite the Shema; and it appears that according to Maimonides as well, this commandment is fulfilled during the recitation of the Shema by accepting His Unity in truth.”
