Kisvei Rav Tzadok Parallel — Meshiv Tzedek, A Rebuttal Regarding Sitting in the Sukkah on Shemini Atzeret 56: “Now certainly the question does not concern a full meal, for how could anyone contemplate permitting that when Scripture states explicitly: “In sukkot shall you dwell seven days”, and the cited dispute of Rabbi Eliezer would be completely irrelevant; rather perforce the intent mirrors that dispute, namely whether one may eat foods not requiring a sukkah throughout the festival, such as fruit and snacks, eating them outside and avoiding the sukkah, or whether one is obligated to eat in the sukkah specifically.”
